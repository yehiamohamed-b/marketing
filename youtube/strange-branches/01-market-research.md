## 4. Proven title patterns
1. `What If [planetary/biological change]?` — *What If Earth Doubled In Size Overnight?*
2. `[X] Years Later` / `+[N] Million Years` — *We Returned to Earth 4,000 Years Later*
3. `What Will Evolve After [event]?`
4. `[Subject] Isn't Done With You` / second person — *Natural Selection Isn't Done With You*
5. `The Strangest [creatures/worlds] That Could [exist/evolve]`

## 5. Strategic conclusions
1. **Pilot = "What If the Dinosaurs Never Died?"** (validated outlier + lowest-competition keyword).
2. Build an **"All Tomorrows"-adjacent series** early (130K searches/mo, competition 34).
3. Mix in **planet-scale what-ifs** monthly for the bigger viral ceiling (The Last Day–style 1M+ views).
4. Use **Gods & Concepts** as the brand differentiator, once a month.
