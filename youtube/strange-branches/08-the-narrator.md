# The Narrator: channel avatar

The Narrator is the power source of every world Strange Branches creates or dissects. He is a god-like presence, not a monster: calm and absolute, able to build a world in his palm and crush it on a whim.

## Design (original character; draws on two reference traits)
| Element | Look | Inspired by |
|---|---|---|
| Silhouette | Tall, slender, pure black humanoid | Mob at 100% (Mob Psycho 100) |
| Eyes | Two glowing white dots in a featureless black head; no mouth | Mob at 100% |
| Skin | Black-and-white maze bands that slowly flow. **Lore:** they are living contour maps of the worlds he has made | God's markings (Versus) |
| Mane | A huge billowing white cloud that flares up when his power rises | God's cloud hair (Versus) |
| Power | A white light sphere behind him, rubble rising, ink flakes peeling off his body | Mob at 100% |
| Accent | **Amber `#e0a020`**: the colour of creation (the world in his palm, the shockwave), matching the logo's eye | Channel brand |

Palette: ink black `#0b0b0d`, paper white `#f6f5ef`, cream `#efe6cf`, amber `#e0a020`, logo green `#1f4a35` (the continents on his world).

## Intro (10 s)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Darkness. Only two eyes open. |
| 1–2 s | The light sphere erupts behind him; his cloud mane flares up. |
| 2–4.5 s | Rubble rises; ink flakes peel off his silhouette. |
| 4.5–6.4 s | He raises a hand; fragments spiral in and form a small amber world with green continents. |
| 6.4–7 s | He crushes it: cracks, implosion, white flash, shockwave, debris blasts outward. |
| 7.5–10 s | Fade to black; his eyes remain above **STRANGE BRANCHES** / *Evolution that never happened*. |

## Outro (10 s)
He stands calm in the light while rubble orbits him. "NEW BRANCH EVERY SATURDAY / the narrator will be waiting" sits underneath. At 8.2 s the light collapses into him and his eyes close last. The left and right thirds stay clear for YouTube end-screen elements.

## Sound brief (CapCut)
- **0–1 s:** a low sub-bass hum.
- **1 s:** a reversed cymbal into a choir swell when the light erupts.
- **2–4.5 s:** grinding stone as the rubble rises.
- **5 s:** a crystalline shimmer as the world forms.
- **6.9 s:** a crunch, then silence for one beat, then a massive impact on the flash.
- **8 s:** a single low piano note under the title.
