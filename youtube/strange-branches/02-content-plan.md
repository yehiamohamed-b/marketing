## Series architecture (for binge-watching)
- **"Mesozoic Forever"** (eps 1, 14, more later): the dinosaur world continues, with recurring named species.
- **"After Us"** (eps 2, 7, 13, 17): the post-human Earth timeline.
- **"Other Skies"** (eps 6, 12, 15, 19): alien worlds.
- **"Divine Zoology"** (eps 4, 9, 16): gods and concepts.
- **"Kaelith Files"**: our original speculative planet. One episode every 4–6 weeks, slotted in from week 6. This is where the heavier AI rendering lives, and the assets are reused across the series.

Each video ends by pointing to the next branch in the same series, which builds playlist chains and session time.

## Shorts system (3–5 per long-form)
1. **Specimen Card** (20–30s): one creature, its stats, one wild adaptation.
2. **Time-Jump** (30–45s): "+10M years → +50M years" transformation montage.
3. **Hook clip** (45–60s): the cold open, ending on "full video on the channel".
4. **Myth vs Biology** (Gods & Concepts only): "Could [mythical creature] actually exist?"

- [ ] Title scored in vidIQ (aim for ≥ 70) and A/B title ready

- [ ] Tags: primary keyword, spec evo, speculative biology, all tomorrows, what if
