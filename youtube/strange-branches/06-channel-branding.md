## 1. Logo (profile picture)

Shows at 98×98 px on the channel page, **as small as 24 px** in comments and search. Design for the small size.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| One bold symbol: a **branching shape that ends in an eye or a spiral**, like a tree of life that turns strange | Words or the full channel name (unreadable at 24 px) |
| 2 colours max on a dark ground: bone-white mark on deep teal/black, plus one **ember or cyan** accent | Photoreal AI art or fine detail |
| A thick silhouette that still reads as a single shape when blurred | Anything that looks like a generic "mystery" or skull channel |
| Centre it inside the circular crop (YouTube crops to a circle) | Elements touching the edges |

**Three concepts to brief (Canva/Magnific/designer):**
1. **The Forked Eye:** a Y-shaped branch whose two tips curl into a single eye, a reference to the tree of life and to Bloodborne's Insight.
2. **The Wrong Tree:** a classic tree-of-life icon where one branch bends at an unnatural angle and ends in a tentacle or claw.
3. **The Specimen Seal:** a circular "museum specimen" stamp with a branching glyph in the centre (the text ring is decoration, not something to read at small size).

**Test:** shrink it to 24 px next to 5 competitor avatars. If you can't pick it out instantly, simplify it.

## 2. Banner

Upload at **2560×1440**, but only the centre **1546×423 "safe area"** shows on every device. Put everything important there.

**Layout:**
- **Left of the safe area:** the "Strange Branches" wordmark (a serif with character, like the Fraunces used in the Evolution Lab, for a "naturalist's journal" feel).
- **Centre/right:** a single wide image showing **one silhouette that evolves along a branching line**, from a normal animal → strange → eldritch. It sells the channel in one glance.
- **Tagline under the wordmark** (pick one):
  - "What if evolution took the other path?"
  - "Speculative biology for the cosmically curious."
  - "The branches life didn't take. Yet."
- **Upload schedule** in small type, e.g. "New branch every Friday", once you have a cadence. It sets expectations.
- **Palette:** deep teal/black background, bone-white text, one ember accent. Use the same colours as thumbnails and the simulator, so everything looks like one brand.

## 3. Channel description (About)

The first ~150 characters show in search. Front-load what the channel is plus a keyword.

**Draft:**
> **What if evolution took the other path?** Strange Branches is speculative biology and "what if" science: alternate Earths, alien ecosystems, impossible creatures, and the real biology behind your favourite game and movie monsters.

Keywords naturally included: speculative biology, speculative evolution, what if, alien ecosystems, All Tomorrows, cosmic horror, creature design.

## 4. Other quick wins
- **Handle and name:** consistent everywhere (@StrangeBranches on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X). Reserve them now.
- **Channel trailer (for non-subscribers):** after 2–3 videos, cut a 45-second trailer from your best hooks.
- **Featured sections:** "Start here" (your best video) → "Alternate Earths" → "Monsters, Made Real" → "Kaelith Files".
- **Video watermark:** the logo mark, bottom right, set to a subscribe button.
- **Consistent thumbnails:** dark background, one subject, one accent colour, ≤4 words. The same look as the banner.
- **Country and language:** set them in YouTube Studio (they're currently blank), so recommendations and RPM targeting work.
