# No Fart: transparent YouTube assets

All PNGs have transparent backgrounds. Cut from the channel's supplied images and Bill Dingus clips with an AI background remover (isnet), then hand-cleaned.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `assets/logo/` | Hand-painted **No Fart** wordmark in blood-red, black and white (1914x760) |
| `assets/icons/` | 12 voxel icons: Godzilla, Mario, Goku, Luffy, Mob, Pikachu, Sonic, Steve, Master Chief, controller, open book, book stack |
| `assets/props/` | Red/black cubes, orbs, spiky mine, ? block, manga volume, What If? bubble, red book stack, ink splat |
| `assets/bill/` | Bill Dingus poses (flex, point, laugh, sad, facepalm, shrug, salute, cup, purple book...) plus front/back turnaround |
| `assets/_contact-sheets/` | Previews of each folder |

## Known limits
- Icons and props come from ~250px crops of the key art, so they suit thumbnails and overlays up to about 500px wide. For hero-size use, re-generate from the same prompt at higher resolution.
- Bill poses are video frames. A few have rough edges around the cloud hair (`bill-point-chest`, `bill-sad`); check on dark and light backgrounds.
- Brand characters (Mario, Pikachu, Sonic, etc.) are third-party IP; keep to transformative/commentary use.
