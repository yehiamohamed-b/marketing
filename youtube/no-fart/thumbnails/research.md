# Thumbnail research: what the sources agree on

Gathered from public web articles (no vidIQ). Almost all are creator-tool or SEO blogs, so treat **figures as directional**; only the patterns that several unrelated sources repeat are used in the templates.

| Pattern | Evidence | How the templates use it |
|---|---|---|
| **One subject, huge, high contrast against the background** | Contrast between subject and background is the most repeated factor ([entreresource](https://entreresource.com/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices/), [thumbli MrBeast guide](https://thumbli.pages.dev/blog/how-mrbeast-designs-his-youtube-thumbnails-template-guide), [touhfa](https://touhfa.art/blog/designtips/mrbeast-thumbnail-article/)) | Bill is near-black on a cream field, so he pops at phone size. Dark variants add a cream cloud glow. |
| **Strong emotion or expression carries the click** | Shock/confusion/excitement named across [entreresource](https://entreresource.com/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices/), [rizzle](https://rizzle.com/blog/post/why-consider-facial-expressions-in-thumbnails), [podcastle](https://podcastle.ai/blog/mrbeast-thumbnails) | Bill has no mouth, so emotion comes from his **pose** (flex, laugh, facepalm, shrug, point). Each template names the pose it needs. |
| **Very few words, big and bold** | 2-5 words in most sources; sources disagree on the exact number ([entreresource](https://entreresource.com/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices/), [thumbli](https://thumbli.pages.dev/blog/how-mrbeast-designs-his-youtube-thumbnails-template-guide)) | Max 4 words, 150px+ type, thick stroke. Title carries the detail. |
| **Hierarchy: subject, then text, then prop** | [entreresource](https://entreresource.com/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices/), [rizzle](https://blog.rizzle.com/post/create-youtube-thumbnails-like-top-youtubers) | Layouts are three-layer: Bill / headline / one prop or icon. |
| **Faceless channels: a recurring character, colour and layout stand in for a face** | [wildandfreetools](https://wildandfreetools.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-ideas-faceless-channel/), [fluxnote brand guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/faceless-channel-brand-identity-guide), [overseeros](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/how-to-brand-a-faceless-youtube-channel) | Same red wordmark corner, same cream/red/black palette, same cube border. |
| **Animated/illustrated characters work when the expression is simple and strong** | [brandmag](https://www.brandmag.net/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2025-what-gets-clicks/) (Kurzgesagt example) | Bill's silhouette + glowing eyes is the "simplified face". |
| **Colour coding per topic, consistent across the channel** | [brandmag](https://www.brandmag.net/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2025-what-gets-clicks/) | Each template has an accent swap (red / amber / violet) for series colour-coding. |
| **Technical: 1280x720, under 2 MB, bottom-right covered by the timestamp, keep key content about 10% inside the edges, text readable at ~20% scale** | [pixelbatch](https://pixelbatch.io/blog/youtube-thumbnail-size-guide), [skysnail](https://skysnail.io/blog/youtube-thumbnail-size-and-safe-zones-2026), [hooksnap](https://www.hooksnap.io/blog/youtube-thumbnail-size-design-guide-2026) | Exports are 1280x720 JPG (under 2 MB). The bottom-right 15% is kept free of text. |
| **Test 2-3 variants** | [growthos](https://growthos.in/blog/youtube-thumbnail-best-practices), [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/faceless-video-thumbnail-guide) | Every template has an A/B variant (swap pose or colour). |

## Gaps
No source analysed the thumbnails of anime/"what if" video-essay channels directly (searches for Kurzgesagt, Gigguk, Mother's Basement, Super Eyepatch Wolf returned nothing on thumbnails). The niche conventions below are therefore **my inference, not sourced**: a recognisable pop-culture icon doing the "hook", a big question or claim, and a single clear emotion.
